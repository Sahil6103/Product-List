HRM (Human Resource Management) Modules:
1. Employee Management
Fields:

id (Auto-generated ID or UUID)

firstName (string)

lastName (string)

email (string, unique)

phoneNumber (string)

position (string)

salary (number)

department (string)

dateOfJoining (date)

status (active/inactive)

address (string)

Role & Permissions:

Admin: Can create, update, delete, and view employees.

HR: Can create, update, and view employees.

Employee: Can view own profile.

Pagination: Display a paginated list of employees.

Filtering: Filter by department, position, or status.

2. Attendance Management
Fields:

id (Auto-generated ID or UUID)

employeeId (foreign key to employee)

date (date)

status (Present/Absent)

remarks (string)

Role & Permissions:

Admin: Can view and manage attendance records.

HR: Can view and mark attendance.

Employee: Can view own attendance records.

Pagination: Paginate attendance data per employee or date.

Filtering: Filter by employee, date range, or attendance status.

3. Leave Management
Fields:

id (Auto-generated ID or UUID)

employeeId (foreign key to employee)

leaveType (Sick Leave, Vacation, etc.)

startDate (date)

endDate (date)

status (Pending/Approved/Rejected)

reason (string)

Role & Permissions:

Admin: Can approve/reject leave requests.

HR: Can approve/reject leave requests.

Employee: Can request leaves and view leave history.

Pagination: Paginate leave requests by employee or date.

Filtering: Filter by leave status, date range, or leave type.

4. Payroll Management
Fields:

id (Auto-generated ID or UUID)

employeeId (foreign key to employee)

salaryMonth (date)

basicSalary (number)

bonus (number)

deductions (number)

netSalary (number)

paymentDate (date)

Role & Permissions:

Admin: Can view and modify salary data.

HR: Can view payroll records.

Employee: Can view own salary slips.

Pagination: Paginate payroll records.

Filtering: Filter by month, employee, or salary range.

5. Role and Permission Management
Fields:

id (Auto-generated ID or UUID)

roleName (Admin, HR, Employee, etc.)

permissions (array of permission strings like create, update, delete, view)

Role & Permissions:

Admin: Can create roles and assign permissions.

HR: Can assign roles and permissions to employees.

Pagination: Paginate roles and permissions.

Filtering: Filter by role name or permissions.

CRM (Customer Relationship Management) Modules:
1. Customer Management
Fields:

id (Auto-generated ID or UUID)

firstName (string)

lastName (string)

company (string)

email (string)

phoneNumber (string)

address (string)

status (Active/Inactive)

createdAt (date)

Role & Permissions:

Admin: Can create, update, delete, and view customers.

Sales: Can create and update customers.

Support: Can view customer details.

Pagination: Paginate customer list.

Filtering: Filter by company, status, or customer name.

2. Lead Management
Fields:

id (Auto-generated ID or UUID)

customerId (foreign key to customer)

leadSource (Referral, Website, etc.)

leadStage (New, In Progress, Converted, Lost)

assignedTo (salesperson)

createdAt (date)

updatedAt (date)

Role & Permissions:

Admin: Can view and manage leads.

Sales: Can create, update, and manage leads.

Pagination: Paginate leads list by stage.

Filtering: Filter by lead stage, salesperson, or date range.

3. Sales/Opportunity Management
Fields:

id (Auto-generated ID or UUID)

leadId (foreign key to lead)

opportunityStage (Initial Contact, Negotiation, Closed)

dealAmount (number)

expectedCloseDate (date)

assignedTo (salesperson)

status (Open/Closed)

Role & Permissions:

Admin: Can view and manage opportunities.

Sales: Can create, update, and close opportunities.

Pagination: Paginate opportunities by stage.

Filtering: Filter by deal stage, amount, or salesperson.

4. Task and Activity Management
Fields:

id (Auto-generated ID or UUID)

taskName (string)

taskDescription (string)

assignedTo (salesperson or support)

dueDate (date)

status (Pending/In Progress/Completed)

createdAt (date)

Role & Permissions:

Admin: Can assign tasks to any employee.

Sales: Can create and manage their tasks.

Support: Can manage tasks related to customer support.

Pagination: Paginate tasks.

Filtering: Filter by task status, assigned user, or due date.

5. Customer Communication (Email/Call Tracking)
Fields:

id (Auto-generated ID or UUID)

customerId (foreign key to customer)

communicationType (Email/Call/Meeting)

communicationDate (date)

details (string)

assignedTo (salesperson or support)

Role & Permissions:

Admin: Can view and manage communication logs.

Sales: Can log communication details.

Support: Can view communication logs.

Pagination: Paginate communication records.

Filtering: Filter by communication type, customer, or date range.

6. Role and Permission Management
Fields:

id (Auto-generated ID or UUID)

roleName (Admin, Sales, Support, etc.)

permissions (array of permission strings like create, update, delete, view)

Role & Permissions:

Admin: Can create roles and assign permissions.

Sales: Can view own roles and permissions.

Pagination: Paginate roles and permissions.

Filtering: Filter by role name or permissions.

General Features (Across HRM & CRM):
Authentication & Authorization

Fields:

userId (foreign key to employee/customer)

username (string)

passwordHash (string)

role (Admin, HR, Sales, etc.)

Role & Permissions:

Admin: Can create and manage users, assign roles.

HR/Sales: Can view own profile and access specific modules based on role.

Audit Logs

Fields:

id (Auto-generated ID)

action (Create, Update, Delete, View)

entityType (Employee, Customer, Lead, etc.)

entityId (ID of the entity)

performedBy (userID of the person who performed the action)

timestamp (date)

Role & Permissions:

Admin: Can view and manage audit logs.
